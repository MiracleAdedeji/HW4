# Campus Customs Design

The design is meant to make shoppers feel excited and ready to buy without feeling overwhelmed or confused.

- I used a clean blue-and-white Bulldog palette with small gold accents to create school spirit while keeping the pages bright and easy to read.
- I added bold headings, clear product cards, organized spacing, and simple navigation so shoppers can quickly find what they want.
- I added product images, prices, short descriptions, category filters, and search so shoppers can compare items without digging through clutter.
- I added subtle motion, hover effects, and a moving campus-style banner to make the storefront feel cool and alive without distracting from the products.
- I added chat assistance and quick prompts so shoppers can get help finding products quickly.
- I kept product details and stock information visible so shoppers can make confident buying decisions.

These choices should help customers stick around because the site feels exciting, organized, and easy to explore. Campus Customs offers variety for different shoppers—whether someone wants a sweatshirt for a student or a gift for their grandma, there is something for them. The product variety, school spirit, chat help, and future deals give customers more reasons to keep browsing and buy from Campus Customs.
